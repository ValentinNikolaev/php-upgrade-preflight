# Outcome decision review

Quick council: three separate Contrarian, First-Principles and Executor passes, followed by one fresh pass reviewing anonymized responses. All used the same bounded frame and GPT-6.1-Sol with Fast/Ultrafast disabled. The passes are analytically separate but correlated; agreement is not independent empirical confirmation. This file is the coordinator's synthesis, not a transcript or a participant result.

The decision concerned presenting this campaign, preparing R3 and deciding whether another broad public-app series was necessary. The frame included the [frozen results](results.md), four semantic defect families, warm ownership failures, selection/cap limits, immutable historical artifacts, missing human observations and the requirement for final review/CI. Component status in the frame was a checkpoint; subsequent exact acceptance belongs to the [engineering record](engineering-review.md).

## Where the council converges

The campaign supports bounded engineering observations and artifact publication. More application counts alone cannot establish human comprehension, planning benefit, voluntary reuse, runtime compatibility or sustainable maintenance. R3 remains unobserved, R4 partial and R5 VALIDATE_FIRST. Historical reports must remain separate from corrected future study stimuli.

## Where the council disagrees

The advisors favored targeted closure rather than another broad series. The anonymous reviewer challenged any categorical conclusion that broader repetition is unnecessary: a demonstrated failure or a broader intended claim could require it. The reviewer also separated publication from study readiness; a reviewed fixed cohort is necessary before ordinary human-study use, but its absence need not prevent qualified disclosure of historical measurements. The coordinator adopts this distinction.

## Blind spots and evidence gaps

Before generating a human-study cohort, define case selection, semantic-review coverage, key adjudication and a defect-triggered stopping rule. Reusing a convenient corrected subset without these decisions would not establish representativeness. General recall, omitted roots, successful six-hop cost, historical OOM cause, actual maintenance consumption and user benefit remain unresolved. Public hashes can be checked; excluded private execution/cleanup records remain attested.

## Weakest points

The leading option relies on bounded semantic review after four attribution/provenance families escaped structural checks. Even reviewed fixed reports would establish suitability only for their stated exercise scope. This requires explicit limits and separate study-material acceptance; it does not block publication of clearly labeled defective historical reports. The warm repair additionally requires offline ownership/immutability/recovery evidence and version-1 compatibility, without reinterpreting the old timings.

## Recommendation

Complete component and aggregate review/CI, then present the qualified historical engineering record. Do not repeat the whole public corpus merely to increase its size. Add a targeted experiment when a named unresolved hypothesis or defect requires it; broaden only when its scope warrants broader coverage. Do not start ordinary R3 participation with the known-defect baseline reports, claim observed benefit or approve a release from these results.

## First action

Finish the versioned warm-cache component's independent review and exact-head checks, then finalize the aggregation acceptance package. Keep fixed-cohort design and actual participant collection as explicit next work under the unchanged [R3 criteria](../decision-study.md#predeclared-decision-criteria).

## What would change the verdict

A residual attribution family, decision-changing omitted source roots, or a reproducible repaired-harness failure would justify a focused extension. A demonstrated general failure could justify a broader series. Reviewed fixed stimuli and actual paired intended-user observations would support a later R3 decision; neither council agreement nor more archived reports can supply them.
