# Public Laravel corpus evaluation

**Frozen measurements complete.** All 37 declared slots produced reports across ten independent applications and one supplemental historical Monica snapshot. The 30 primary runs returned 27 blocked and three unknown state pairs; all slots together returned 30 blocked and seven unknown pairs. This is an engineering evaluation record, not evidence of successful application upgrades or completed R3/R4 readiness.

The measured tool baseline is `6a2251901725321d4206e008ac42205b32ddfa8d`, schema `0.8`. The [source pins](projects.json), [blinded source oracle](manual-oracle.md) and [environment](environment.json) identify the inputs and execution context. Three earlier diagnostic attempts are separate from the 37-slot series; see the protocol for their cancellation/measurement limitations.

## What the measurements mean

`analysis_seconds` times `analyzeUpgrade()` and excludes report rendering. PHP heap peak and the analyzer's own `/proc/self/status` VmHWM are collected at shutdown, so they include rendering. External wall time includes the harness invocation, its hashing/startup/raw-output copy/cleanup and the controller's post-run source verification. Controller intake verification and cache preparation/copying precede that timer and remain unmeasured, as do acquisition, installation, review and pauses. These observations are not a user planning-time comparison.

Composer starts are observed wrapper invocations, including version/validation/scenario/diagnostic commands. Fault probes can delay or stop that PHP process before normal Composer logic executes. Timed records are shutdown resource observations, not successful solves. Canonical diagnostic-entry counts include reused entries and must not be summed as unique processes. Per-invocation resource exit status or signal may be unknown even when the canonical scenario records its own exit status.

Composer RSS with `composer_php_process` scope is its PHP process's own VmHWM. The GNU-time analyzer envelope can include waited descendants; neither is a concurrent RSS sum. The container cgroup peak is a separate measurement. Missing or interrupted observations remain null. Heap, RSS and container peaks must not be combined or treated as interchangeable.

Cold means an initially empty dedicated Composer cache. Warm used a byte-verified independent host copy of the preceding cold cache. That copy did not preserve Linux ownership: Lychee, Snipe-IT and Invoice Ninja warm runs encountered Git dubious-ownership refusals. Their timings remain archived but are excluded from ordinary warm comparisons. A separately reviewed versioned harness repair is integrated; post-repair public-application performance is unmeasured. Live registry drift and shared host activity also prevent causal cache-speedup or isolated-machine claims. Target platform assumptions are simulated dependency inputs, not a tested application runtime.

The shipped 256 MiB memory, 512 KiB JSON and 256 KiB Markdown targets are advisory; see the [contract](../../v0.3-contract.md#platform-and-execution-boundary). This campaign's shorter timeouts, 1024 MiB normal analyzer/Composer limits and 2 GiB container limit are declared evaluation settings, not new product guarantees. Source omission evidence and absent vendor/application validation leave recall and runtime compatibility unknown.

## Known findings and remaining gates

Independent reviews identified four attribution/provenance defect families, with exact source/report pointers in the case reviews. [PR 45](https://github.com/ValentinNikolaev/php-upgrade-preflight/pull/45) repairs the first three; [PR 47](https://github.com/ValentinNikolaev/php-upgrade-preflight/pull/47) separates candidate versions from proven locked versions. [PR 46](https://github.com/ValentinNikolaev/php-upgrade-preflight/pull/46) adds the portable publisher and export regressions. PRs 45, 46, 47 and 48 have separate approval, all 19 Quality jobs successful at their respective final heads, and are merged only into the aggregation branch. PR 48 repairs runtime ownership with offline Git/copy/recovery checks; its version-2 publisher projection remains deferred. The [aggregate PR 44](https://github.com/ValentinNikolaev/php-upgrade-preflight/pull/44) requires a fresh final review and exact-head CI; the maintainer owns the final merge. Baseline reports stay unchanged; regression tests do not establish post-repair public performance or complete causal interpretation of every candidate/advisory diagnostic.

The 37 invocations total 68.4 minutes of external time. Maximum primary analyzer heap was 201.4 MiB, and maximum primary JSON was 7.1 MiB. Bagisto's conventional default source selection omitted its 31 Webkul source roots; its smaller resource figures do not profile the whole application. Other applications often reached the 10,000-usage cap. Source recall remains unknown.

The Monica 7 six-hop request stopped at its first transition, with five later stages skipped. That does not measure successful six-hop candidate chaining. R3 participant comprehension, paired planning benefit, voluntary reuse and Symfony-owner cases remain unavailable. The conservative [VALIDATE_FIRST direction](../release-direction.md) remains in place; this study authorizes no release tag, schema change or Symfony implementation.
