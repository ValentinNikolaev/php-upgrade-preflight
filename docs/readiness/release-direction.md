# R5 release direction: validate first

Decision recorded 2026-10-10 against the published v0.3.5, schema `0.8` baseline. **Defer the Symfony adapter and retain the current Laravel/PHP/Composer planning focus.** This is a conservative scope decision, not a finding that Symfony demand is absent or that user value has been disproved. It authorizes no v0.4 contract migration, fourth package, new branch, version, tag, or maintenance release. The [active plan](../../.claude/DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) owns the next gates.

## Decision criteria and evidence

| Criterion | Status at decision | Evidence and consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Input isolation and report clarity | **Passed in development** | R1's real offline Composer regression proved and repaired ambient `COMPOSER` manifest redirection in compatible and restricted modes. R2 qualified risk, effort, budgets and next actions without changing schema `0.8`; focused tests, static checks, coverage and 18 selective mutants passed. The [baseline](decision-baseline.md) records the composed checks and their limit: a final uninterrupted `composer check` had not yet passed after the last R2 fixes. Neither change is published by this decision. |
| Reader comprehension and decision benefit | **Unavailable** | The [predeclared study](decision-study.md) has no participants, paired answers/times, critical-claim observations, or voluntary second-decision reuse. Its four-of-five comprehension, zero-critical, 20% median time benefit without quality loss, and three-team reuse thresholds cannot be scored. Missing observations are not zero incidents or failed demand. |
| Symfony owner need | **Unavailable** | No two rooted Symfony-owner upgrade cases identify a hop, component constraint, manual/recipe work and useful read-only decision. The proposed pair and application shape therefore have no user-case basis. |
| Operational cost and capacity | **Partially observed; gate open** | [R4](operational-cost.md) measured real Composer on two small offline fixtures and separate three-package CI timing proxies. A real large/worst-stage application, child-process RSS, the cause of Lychee's 128 MiB failure, live-network cost, maintainer capacity and user setup/read benefit are unavailable. Advisory 256 MiB and report-size targets are not enforced guarantees. |
| Contract expansion and release feasibility | **Unavailable** | Without owner cases or capacity, there is no justified schema `0.9` migration, same-major stage identity, family-scoped targets, multi-provider implementation, fourth-package matrix or fifth Wiki destination. Existing v0.3 safety and release gates still apply. |

Confidence is **high in the decision to defer a new public contract and maintenance commitment now**, because the prerequisites are absent, and **low in any prediction of future user demand or optimal release theme**. The benefit is keeping engineering effort on a published, testable planning job and avoiding a broad contract change before readers validate it. The cost is delayed Symfony coverage and delayed architectural proof from a second published adapter. Continued v0.3.x maintenance must preserve schema `0.8`, the PHP `^8.0` runtime floor, current supported transitions, and the documented read-only/evidence behavior. This decision does not claim that the R1/R2 development changes are shipped.

## Next evidence and change conditions

1. Have the maintainer arrange the ten-active-working-day R3 comparison: five paired studies from at least three independent intended-user teams, with all predeclared comprehension, safety, planning-time and observed-reuse results recorded. Record a failed or incomplete study honestly. No agent outreach is implied.
2. Reproduce pinned Lychee under declared input, PHP/Composer, network and cache state, separating analyzer peak from child RSS and testing a failed/successful memory-limit pair. Profile a representative large request and real worst supported stage chain; record resource and cleanup outcomes. Determine maintainer capacity, fourth-package/Wiki upkeep and actual user setup/run/read time before estimating net benefit.
3. Seek at least two concrete Symfony-owner cases. Reconsider an adapter only if those cases show a decision the local read-only report improves, the R3 safety and benefit assessment is reviewable, and operational/release cost fits an explicit maintainer budget. A different demonstrated Laravel/PHP/Composer need may justify a narrower release first.
4. Before any schema or adapter implementation, Milestone 0 must approve an exact contract from those cases. Current v0.3 behavior refuses multiple active stage providers, and the Laravel stage planner skips unsupported mixed Laravel-family targets with evidence. Future competing rooted-family ownership and repeated `--framework` selections require an explicit contract. If an owner case requires same-major/minor precision or family staging, define only that case's version, target, attribution and migration rules. Structured completeness, nullability and enforced-versus-advisory budget fields in schema `0.9` are candidates for that contract, not permission to implement them now.

R3 and R4 empirical acceptance remain **open**. This R5 direction closes the immediate scope decision while leaving the broader readiness milestone incomplete. The original [point-in-time audit](../../.claude/audits/2026-10-10-product-engineering-review.md) remains historical evidence; this record states the later decision.
